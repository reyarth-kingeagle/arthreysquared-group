Privacy and marking controls

  • No account login: The page is public. An API key is still required to call the external AI service.
  • No application storage: The app does not upload files to GitHub, store them in a database, or save results between visits.
  • Third-party processing: Photo content is sent to the configured AI provider. Its data-retention rules still apply; this is not a zero-retention guarantee.
  • Handwriting uncertainty: Unclear text or names must be checked against the original paper. The tool never promises perfect transcription or grading.
  • Rubric-anchored: Essay marks are allocated to the configured instructor rubric criteria, not to multiple-choice answers.
  • Instructor decision: Scores are provisional and require your review before they are used in a class record.

Your selected image files and temporary results are cleared from this page by the Clear button or by closing the page. This browser does not store a grading history.
