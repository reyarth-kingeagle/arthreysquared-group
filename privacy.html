Last updated: November 15, 2026

No student enrollment on this website

ARTHREYSQUARED Group offers publicly accessible educational pages. This website does not provide student account creation, sign-in, enrollment forms, or requests for student IDs, grades, institutional email addresses, or passwords.

Visitors can browse published educational materials without identifying themselves to ARTHREYSQUARED Group through a website registration form.

Academic records

Student rosters, graded assessments, and class records are not managed through this public website. Academic records should be handled only through institution-approved channels with appropriate access controls.

Hosting and external services

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Hosting providers and linked third-party websites may process standard technical information, such as IP addresses, browser details, and access logs, according to their own policies. Clicking a link to an external service takes you outside this website.

Contact and privacy questions

For questions about this notice, use the official contact options on the Contact page. Please do not send confidential student records, IDs, passwords, or grades through public contact channels.
